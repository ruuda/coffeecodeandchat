Coffee, Code & Chat

Meetup in Utrecht

We’re a meetup of tech enthusiasts around Utrecht, NL. We get together every other week on Saturday morning, get a coffee, and chat about code.

Anybody is welcome, whether you’re a hobbyist just getting into programming or an industry veteran, regardless of the languages and technologies you work with.

Location

Lebkov, at Utrecht Centraal, at the side of Hoog Catharijne. Usually we sit at one of the two tables in the back. WiFi and power sockets are available here.

We have an Eventbrite page and a page on meetup.com. Attendance on Meetup is not always accurate, some of us have been going here for years and don’t always update the status on Meetup. Usually there are 3–5 of us, on busy days as many as 10.
